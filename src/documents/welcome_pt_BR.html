Obrigado por utilizar o QPrompt!

O QPrompt elimina o estresse da operação do teleprompter, permitindo que você aproveite a produção de mídia e evite ter que memorizar textos.

Nosso objetivo com o QPrompt é facilitar a operação de um teleprompter, ao mesmo tempo em que maximizamos a produtividade, o desempenho e a precisão do controle.

QPrompt is free software as defined by the FSF; meaning you're free to share, study, and modify QPrompt to your needs.

Visit the website qprompt.app for instructional materials and to download the latest version of QPrompt. You can also contribute to QPrompt's development yourself and help translate the program and its resources at l10n.qprompt.app. Visit our community section at qprompt.app for more ways to contribute.

QPrompt is pronounced "Q" "Prompt". The name is short for Cue Prompter, something said to cue teleprompter operators. Also, QPrompt is built using the Qt framework. The Q is meant to reference all of these things plus the word "quick", because teleprompter operators must be quick to respond to what happens on set.

QPrompt is developed and maintained by Javier Cordero, also known as Cuperino, with the help of users like you, who share valuable feedback, translate QPrompt, and help test betas.
